## What is communication?

Communication is the process of **exchanging information, ideas and feelings** between a sender and a receiver so that both reach a shared understanding.

## The communication process

1. **Sender:** has an idea and starts the communication.
2. **Encoding:** turns the idea into words, symbols or gestures.
3. **Message:** the encoded content.
4. **Channel:** the medium, such as speech, email, a report or a video call.
5. **Receiver:** the person or group the message is meant for.
6. **Decoding:** the receiver interprets the message.
7. **Feedback:** the receiver's response, which closes the loop.

**Noise** is anything that distorts the message at any stage.

## Levels of communication

- **Intrapersonal:** with oneself, such as thinking or planning.
- **Interpersonal:** between two people.
- **Group:** within a small team.
- **Mass:** to a large audience through the media.

## Barriers to communication

| Type | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Physical | Noise, distance, poor network |
| Semantic | Jargon, ambiguous words, translation issues |
| Psychological | Stress, prejudice, lack of attention |
| Cultural | Different customs, gestures or values |
| Organisational | Rigid hierarchy, too many layers |

### Overcoming barriers

- Know your audience and adapt your language.
- Keep the message **clear, concise and complete**.
- Choose the right channel for the message.
- Encourage and act on feedback.
- Listen actively.

## The 7 Cs of effective communication

**Clear, Concise, Concrete, Correct, Coherent, Complete and Courteous.**
