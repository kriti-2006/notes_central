## Value education

**Value education** helps us understand what is truly valuable for human happiness and how to achieve it. It complements skills: **values tell us what to do, and skills tell us how to do it.**

## Self-exploration

Self-exploration is the process of finding out what is valuable to me by investigating **within myself**. It involves:

1. **Natural acceptance:** what feels right deep down, consistently, for everyone, at all times.
2. **Experiential validation:** testing that understanding in real life, in my behaviour and work.

It is a dialogue between "what I am" and "what I naturally want to be".

## Basic human aspirations

Every human being aspires to **continuous happiness and prosperity**.

- **Happiness:** being in a state of harmony, without contradiction inside oneself.
- **Prosperity:** the feeling of having **more than enough** physical facilities.

## The basic requirements

To fulfil these aspirations we need all three of the following:

| Requirement | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Right understanding | Clarity about oneself and one's relationships |
| Relationship | Feelings of trust and respect with other people |
| Physical facilities | Food, clothing, shelter and other material needs |

Ignoring right understanding and relationships while chasing only physical facilities leads to **deprivation**: having things, but still feeling unhappy.

## The human being as self and body

- The **self ('I')** is the conscious entity. Its needs are happiness and trust, they are **qualitative**, and they are fulfilled by right understanding and feelings.
- The **body** is the material entity. Its needs are physical facilities, they are **quantitative**, and they are fulfilled by physical means.

Many problems arise when we try to satisfy the needs of the self with physical facilities, for example seeking respect through possessions.

## Harmony in the self

The self is in harmony when its desires, thoughts and expectations are guided by **right understanding**. Sanyam (self-regulation) is how the self takes responsibility for nurturing, protecting and making right use of the body.
