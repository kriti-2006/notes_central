## Harmony in the family

The family is the **basic unit of human interaction** and the place where we first learn relationships.

### The nine feelings (values) in relationships

**Trust, respect, affection, care, guidance, reverence, glory, gratitude and love.**

- **Trust** is the foundational value: being assured that the other person intends my happiness.
- **Respect** means right evaluation: accepting the other person as being like me.

### Intention vs competence

We judge **ourselves by our intentions** but **others by their competence**. Most conflicts start from this mismatch. Recognising that everyone intends to be happy builds trust.

## Harmony in society

Harmony grows from the family to the whole of society. The goals of a harmonious society are:

1. **Right understanding** in every individual
2. **Prosperity** in every family
3. **Fearlessness** (trust) in society
4. **Co-existence** with nature

### The five dimensions of a human order

| Dimension | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Education and sanskar | Develops right understanding |
| Health and sanyam | Takes care of the body |
| Production and work | Produces physical facilities |
| Justice and preservation | Maintains relationships and protects nature |
| Exchange and storage | Shares what is produced, rather than exploiting others |

## Harmony in nature

Nature consists of four orders:

| Order | Examples | Innateness |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Material order | Soil, water, metals | Composition and decomposition |
| Plant order | Trees, plants | Composition, decomposition and respiration |
| Animal order | Animals, birds | Adds the selection of food and shelter |
| Human order | Human beings | Adds knowing, assuming and recognising |

The first three orders are naturally **mutually fulfilling**. Human beings are the only order that can disturb this cycle, through excessive consumption and pollution, and also the only order that can consciously **restore** it.

## Professional ethics

Holistic human values lead to ethical professional conduct: competence combined with **right understanding**, so that work fulfils people and nature instead of exploiting them.
