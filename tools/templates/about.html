A better home for what you learn

Notes Central is an independently developed educational platform created to make academic resources easier to discover, access, and navigate.

Product vision

Study material should feel like a library, not a scavenger hunt

The idea behind Notes Central is simple: every subject gets a home, every resource is one search away, and the things you care about are waiting for you when you come back.

Instead of another folder of files, it’s a connected learning space. You can explore by subject and topic, read in a reader designed for revision, and keep your own library of saved notes and reading history.

Your knowledge deserves a better home.

The problem

Notes are everywhere, except where you need them

Before every exam the same question comes up: “Does anyone have the notes?” Material ends up scattered across group chats, drives and phone photos, with no way to search it and no way to tell what exists.

Notes Central fixes that by organising notes by year and subject, making them searchable by name or short form, and presenting them in a format that’s comfortable to read on any device, including a phone on the way to an exam.

Core functionality

What it does

  • 01

    Smart resource explorer

    Search titles, subjects, topics and short forms; filter by subject, type and category; sort; share any view by link.

  • 02

    A reader for revision

    Table of contents, reading progress, adjustable text size, reveal-one-at-a-time practice questions, download and print.

  • 03

    Your personal library

    Bookmarks, recently viewed resources and favourite subjects, saved instantly on your device.

  • 04

    Accounts that sync

    Sign in and your library is stored with your account, so it follows you between devices.

  • 05

    Quick find

    A keyboard-first command palette on every page, opened with / or CtrlK.

  • 06

    Light and dark themes

    Two complete colour themes, each checked for contrast, that follow your system until you choose.

Technology & engineering

Fast, framework-free, and built to last

The frontend is semantic HTML, a single CSS design system built on custom properties, and small vanilla JavaScript modules, with no frameworks and no build step required to run it. Resources are described in a JSON catalogue and written in Markdown, which the reader renders safely in the browser.

Accounts and library sync run on a Node.js and Express API backed by MySQL, with passwords hashed using bcrypt, JSON Web Tokens for sessions, server-side input validation, and secrets kept in environment variables.

  • Node.js

  • JWT & bcrypt

Quality

Designed for everyone, on every screen

  • Accessibility

    Built to WCAG 2.0 AA: semantic structure, a skip link, full keyboard support, visible focus, labelled forms with clear errors, sufficient contrast in both themes, and reduced-motion support.

  • Responsive design

    Mobile-first layouts tested from 320px phones to 1920px displays, with fluid type, adaptive grids and touch-friendly controls, and no horizontal scrolling.

  • User experience

    Helpful empty states, loading skeletons, recoverable errors and subtle motion that explains what changed, without getting in the way.
